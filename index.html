Pair your earbuds with the browser.

Ferusyx talks to your earbuds over Web Bluetooth — the real radio link, not a mock. It shows exactly what your device exposes: battery, identity, and its discovered services. Nothing here is simulated.

Chrome / Edge on desktop or Android
Secure connection (HTTPS)
Bluetooth turned on

Note on scope — Apple AirPods use a closed, encrypted protocol that no website (or non-Apple app) can read. Ferusyx works with earbuds that expose standard Bluetooth LE services — most budget and mid-range earbuds do. If a feature isn't exposed by your hardware, Ferusyx will say so instead of faking it.
